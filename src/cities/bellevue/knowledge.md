# Bellevue CityDesk — what callers can be told

_CityDesk is a demo, not an official City of Bellevue service. Facts below come from bellevuewa.gov (saved 2026-10-17, see `pages/`) and the city's MyBellevue 311 data. Department phone numbers and hours are listed with the departments._

## Hours

- City Hall's Service First desk: weekdays 8 AM to 4 PM, 425-452-6800, 450 110th Ave NE. Interpretation in over 240 languages on the same number; deaf or hard of hearing callers can use 711.
- Mini City Hall: Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM, 425-452-2800 — the in-person option on Saturdays.
- Most department phone lines: weekdays 8 AM to 5 PM.
- City Hall is closed on Veterans Day (November 11), Thanksgiving (November 26) and Christmas Day (December 25).
- Outside office hours CityDesk opens tickets, and the department picks them up when it reopens.

## Emergencies and urgent problems

- Danger to life or safety (fire, crime in progress, medical emergency, gas leak, someone injured or trapped): call 911.
- The city also asks for 911 for malfunctioning traffic signals, downed or damaged stop or yield signs, and a downed tree completely blocking a street.
- Flooding, water main breaks, sewer overflows, pollutant spills and street emergencies: the city's 24-hour line, 425-452-7840.
- Police non-emergency: 425-577-5656 (NORCOM).
- Homelessness: if someone is in danger or needs medical help, call 911. Otherwise the City Manager's Office outreach team follows up on reports.

## Reporting directly to the city

- Residents can also report problems themselves through MyBellevue, the city's app (App Store and Google Play) and online customer assistance portal. Shopping carts, code violations, graffiti, encampments and park issues are all reported there.

## What happens next, by request

- Abandoned shopping carts: usually collected within 4 to 5 days, sometimes up to 14. A cart someone is using or living out of is a homelessness outreach request, not a cart pickup.
- Construction noise is allowed 7 AM to 8 PM Monday to Friday and 8 AM to 6 PM on Saturdays; none on Sundays or legal holidays. Outside those hours it's a code violation for Development Services.
- Private fireworks are illegal in Bellevue; report them to the Fire Department or through MyBellevue.
- Missed garbage, recycling or yard debris pickup: Republic Services, 425-452-4762 (residential) or 425-646-2492 (commercial and multifamily).
- Neighbor disputes: the Conflict Resolution Center, 425-452-4091, free for people who live or work in Bellevue.

## Not handled by the city — who does

When a caller needs one of these, say it isn't a city service and name who handles it (no phone numbers unless listed here):

- Green cards, permanent residence, citizenship and other immigration documents: U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the federal government. USCIS Contact Center: 1-800-375-5283 (TTY 1-800-767-1833), per uscis.gov.
- Driver's licenses, state ID cards and vehicle tabs: the Washington State Department of Licensing.
- Passports: the U.S. Department of State; many post offices accept applications.
- Voter registration and ballots: King County Elections.
- Property tax: King County.
- Pet licenses: King County, 206-296-7387.
- Birth and death certificates: Public Health – Seattle & King County, or the Washington State Department of Health.
- Schools and enrollment: the school district, not the city.

## What people report most

From 34,576 MyBellevue requests (June 2021 to October 2026), most common first: abandoned shopping carts (about 1 in 5), code violations, illegally parked or abandoned vehicles, homelessness assistance, park issues, graffiti, traffic concerns, fireworks, street maintenance and signals and streetlights, trees and overgrown vegetation.

## Tickets

- Every CityDesk ticket gets a reference like CD-1042.
- The department reviews new tickets on its next working day, and contacts the caller on the number they gave when there is an update or the problem is fixed.
- Callers can call back any time, with their reference or from the same phone number, to hear the status.
- Statuses: Reported (received, not yet reviewed), In progress (being looked at), Scheduled (work is booked), Fixed.

## Common questions

- **Events:** search the city's calendar snapshot (search_city_info) and say it's as of its date; the live calendar is on the city's website.
- **Laws and rules** (noise, parking, permits): the Bellevue Municipal Code on the city's website is the official source. CityDesk can't confirm a specific rule over the phone beyond what's written here.
- **Anything else:** search the city's website snapshot first, then the city's website or a ticket to City Hall Customer Service.
