# Bellevue CityDesk — what callers can be told

_Demo knowledge for the CityDesk prototype. Not official City of Bellevue information._

## Hours

- In this demo, city departments answer calls on weekdays from 8 AM to 4 PM Pacific time.
- Outside those hours CityDesk opens tickets, and the department picks them up when it reopens.

## Departments

Bellevue's departments and what they handle, based on the city's MyBellevue 311 requests (June 2021 to October 2026).

- **Transportation** — potholes and street repairs, traffic signals, streetlights, sidewalks, road debris, overgrown vegetation along roads, street signs, traffic concerns, and scooters or bikes parked in the way.
- **Utilities** — water leaks, water quality, drainage and flooding, sewer problems, spills and pollution, garbage and recycling, utility bills.
- **Police Department** (non-emergency) — parking enforcement, abandoned vehicles, traffic enforcement requests, contacting your police sector captain.
- **Development Services** — code violations (property upkeep, construction without permits, zoning, construction noise), graffiti, landmark tree removal.
- **Community Development** — abandoned shopping carts, help resolving conflicts with neighbors.
- **Parks & Community Services** — parks, trails, park trees, playground equipment, trash in parks, pet waste, dogs off leash.
- **City Manager's Office** — homelessness assistance and outreach, including encampments.
- **Fire Department** (non-emergency) — fireworks reports, dry vegetation fire hazards, fire station tours, community event requests.
- **City Hall Customer Service** — general questions and anything that doesn't fit another department.

## What people report most

From 34,576 MyBellevue requests, most common first:

1. Abandoned shopping carts (about 1 in 5 requests) — Community Development.
2. Code violations — Development Services.
3. Illegally parked or abandoned vehicles — Police Department.
4. Homelessness assistance and outreach — City Manager's Office.
5. Park issues — Parks & Community Services.
6. Graffiti — Development Services (Transportation or Utilities when it's on their equipment).
7. Traffic concerns — Transportation, or the Police Department for enforcement.
8. Fireworks — Fire Department.
9. Street maintenance, traffic signals, streetlights — Transportation.
10. Trees and overgrown vegetation — Parks & Community Services in parks, Transportation along roads.

Homelessness: if someone is in danger or needs medical help, call 911. Otherwise the City Manager's Office outreach team follows up.

## Emergencies and urgent problems

- Danger to life or safety (fire, crime in progress, medical emergency, gas leak, someone injured or trapped): call 911.
- Urgent but not life-threatening (for example a water main break, a tree blocking a road, a signal out at a busy intersection): the city's after-hours urgent line, +1 555 0199. This is a demo number in this prototype.

## Tickets

- Every ticket gets a reference like CD-1042.
- The department reviews new tickets on its next working day.
- The department contacts the caller on the number they gave when there is an update or the problem is fixed.
- Callers can call back any time, with their reference or from the same phone number, to hear the status.
- Statuses: Reported (received, not yet reviewed), In progress (being looked at), Scheduled (work is booked), Fixed.

## Common questions

- **Events:** the city's events calendar is on the city's website. CityDesk can't read out live listings yet.
- **Laws and rules** (noise, parking, permits): the Bellevue Municipal Code on the city's website is the official source. CityDesk can't confirm a specific rule over the phone.
- **Anything else:** the city's website, or a ticket to City Hall Customer Service.
