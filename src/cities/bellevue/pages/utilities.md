# Utilities

Bellevue Utilities provides essential services that you rely on every day — drinking water, wastewater, storm and surface water, and solid waste. It is our priority to ensure these services are safe, reliable and high-quality.

### About Bellevue Utilities

 Bellevue Utilities Business Profile outlines our priorities and investment areas needed to ensure safe and reliable utility services in Bellevue for generations to come.

Bellevue Utilities has been accredited by the American Public Works Association (APWA) since 2004. This accreditation formally verifies Bellevue Utilities for compliance with the recommended practices for public works agencies.
