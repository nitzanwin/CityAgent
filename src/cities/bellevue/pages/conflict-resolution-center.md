# Conflict Resolution Center

If you need some help resolving a conflict, the Bellevue Conflict Resolution Center may be able to help you. Our free and confidential services help you build understanding and resolve conflicts with others through coaching, phone conciliation and mediation. These services are available for people who work or live in Bellevue and Kirkland.

Our mission is to help you and your community build your problem-solving capacity. To this end we have trainings, educational resources and volunteer opportunities for you to master the art of conflict resolution and help others.

We can help with a wide range of issues, including neighbor and housing disputes, fences and property lines, noise, trees, landlord and tenant disputes, and cross-cultural issues.

The Conflict Resolution Center is a dispute resolution center formed under Chapter 7.75 of the Revised Code of Washington. It is a member of Resolution WA, a consortium of dispute resolution centers in Washington.
